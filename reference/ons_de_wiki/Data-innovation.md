Awareness: show an awareness of opportunities for innovation with new tools and uses of data

Working: understand the impact on the organisation of emerging trends in data tools, analysis techniques and data usage

Practitioner: identify areas of innovation in data tools and techniques, and recognise appropriate timing for adoption

[Expert](#expert): investigate emerging trends in data-related approaches, perform horizon-scanning for the organisation and introduce innovative ways of working

# General resources

## Community and podcasts

Engaging with the wider data community can prove a useful when it comes to discovering and adopting new tools and technologies. 

The [Data Skeptic Podcast](https://dataskeptic.com/) has hosts from various backgrounds - topic may be more or less relevant each week. 

PyData meetups can be a good way to find out how others are using data in your local area. The four most relevant are [Southampton](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-southampton/), [Cardiff](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-cardiff-meetup/), [Manchester](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-manchester/) and [London](https://pydata.org/london2025). 

# Expert 

## Strategy and leadership

The book Good Strategy, Bad Strategy by Richard Rumelt defines and gives examples of the titles namesake. Here is a good [10 minute summary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vyezDs2KCAE) of the book.

The four stages of data maturity could help leaders to understand how to help their teams and the organisation as a whole become more data driven. Here is a [blog post](https://www.heap.io/blog/the-four-stages-of-data-maturity) that outlines the stages of maturity and (at a high level) how to achieve them. 

## Horizon scanning

[Towards Data Science](https://towardsdatascience.com/) publishes on may topics relevant to data engineers as well as data scientists. 

A possibly useful resource for horizon scanning is [McKinsey - The state of AI](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)

If you are really keen on working at the cutting edge of research you could set up custom alerts on [arxiv.org](arxiv.org).
