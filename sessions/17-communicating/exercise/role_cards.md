# Stakeholder role cards

Cut these up, or have the stakeholder pick a number without looking.

---

### 1. The Deputy Director (Education Analysis)
You have **5 minutes** before your next meeting. You need to tell the minister's office whether Thursday publication is safe. You don't care how it works. You care about **risk** and **reputation**.
**Ask:** "So can we publish on Thursday or not?" · "What's the worst that could happen?"

---

### 2. The Statistician / Methodologist
You own the **method**. You're worried the pipeline will calculate rates differently from your spreadsheet, and you've been burned before by "black box" systems.
**Ask:** "How exactly is the rate calculated?" · "What happens to a school that changes phase mid-week?" · "Can I check it?"

---

### 3. The Local Authority Data Contact
You send the daily files. You're **busy** and slightly **defensive**: last time someone complained about your data, it turned out to be their mistake.
**Ask:** "What do you need from us, exactly?" · "Why does it matter if we're a day late?" · "Who do I contact?"

---

### 4. The DisCO (Disclosure Control Officer)
You'll sign off the outputs. You care about **small numbers** and whether any school could be identified.
**Ask:** "What's the smallest cell?" · "How do you stop differencing between weeks?" · "When will I see a draft?"

---

### 5. The New Team Member (an analyst, not an engineer)
You've just joined and will **run** this pipeline when the engineer is on leave. You're nervous.
**Ask:** "What do I do if it fails on a Thursday morning?" · "How do I know the numbers are right?"

---

### 6. The Platform / Cloud Migration Lead
You're planning next year's **CDP → GCP** move. You want to know how hard this pipeline will be to migrate.
**Ask:** "What's platform-specific?" · "Is it scripted end to end or does someone click things?" · "Does it handle being re-run?"
