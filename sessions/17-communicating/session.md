## Learning outcomes
By the end of the session, participants can:
1. Identify what different stakeholders care about and adapt the message to them.
2. Translate technical concepts into plain English without losing accuracy.
3. Structure a message with the answer first (pyramid principle / BLUF).
4. Gather requirements by asking good questions and playing back what they heard.
5. Communicate risks, bad news and uncertainty honestly and early.
6. Raise concerns with someone more senior using graded assertiveness (PACE).
7. Know the basics of presenting in slides, reports and posters, accessibly.

## Exercise
- **Task:** de-jargon five sentences, then explain the session 15 pipeline in 3 minutes to three different stakeholder personas, with peer feedback.
- **Starting files:** `exercise/README.md`, `exercise/role_cards.md`, `exercise/feedback_form.md`
- **Done when:** everyone has explained to at least two personas and received feedback.
- **Reference:** `solutions/README.md` (example translations and openings, debrief points)

## Facilitator notes
- Keep the concept part short (30 min). The role-play is where the learning happens.
- Encourage stakeholders to stay in role and interrupt. A polite audience doesn't help anyone practise.
- Some people find role-play uncomfortable. Offer the observer role first, and keep feedback to "one thing that worked, one thing to try".
