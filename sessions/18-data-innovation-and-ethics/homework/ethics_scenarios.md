# Ethics scenarios (used in session, 10 min in pairs)

For each one: **Is there an ethical issue? Which principle? What would you do?**

1. **The helpful assistant.** A colleague pastes 200 rows of a survey extract (with postcodes and dates of birth) into a public AI chatbot to "help write the cleaning code faster".

2. **The clever linkage.** You realise you could link the school attendance data to a health dataset you also have access to, which would make a much richer analysis. Nobody asked for it, and the data sharing agreement covers "education statistics".

3. **The quiet fix.** You find that a published series has been 3% too high for six months because of a join bug. Fixing it means a revision. Your manager suggests fixing it "going forward" without announcing it.

4. **The proxy.** A model to prioritise business survey follow-up uses postcode as a feature. It works well, but it means businesses in deprived areas are chased far more often.

5. **The synthetic shortcut.** To speed up testing, a team proposes copying production data to the dev environment "because synthetic data never has the real problems".

6. **The new shiny.** A vendor offers a free trial of a cloud data-quality tool. It needs read access to your Hive tables to "auto-profile everything".
