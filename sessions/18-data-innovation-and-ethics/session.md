## Learning outcomes
By the end of the session, participants can:
1. Explain why innovation matters for a public sector data team, and what makes it different there.
2. Apply data ethics principles (UKSA ethics principles, the Data Ethics Framework) to a scenario.
3. Evaluate a new tool against fit, maturity, integration, cost, security and exit cost.
4. Judge the **timing** of adoption: adopt, trial, assess or hold.
5. Use horizon scanning and communities of practice to keep up to date without chasing every trend.

## Homework
- **Task:** evaluate one tool or technique with `evaluation_template.md`, including a hands-on trial on synthetic data and an Adopt / Trial / Assess / Hold recommendation.
- **Starting files:** `homework/README.md`, `homework/evaluation_template.md`, `homework/ethics_scenarios.md`
- **Done when:** see the checklist in `homework/README.md`
- **Reference:** `solutions/example_evaluation.md` (Polars, plus facilitator notes for the ethics scenarios)
- **Share-back:** 2-minute summaries at a team meeting or community of practice. These can seed a team tech radar.
